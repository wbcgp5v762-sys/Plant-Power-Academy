Three ideas you need

Voltage (V) is the difference in electrical potential that drives charge through a circuit. Think of pressure difference in a water pipe.

Current (A) is the rate electric charge flows. Think of how much water passes a point each second.

Resistance (Ω) limits current. Think of a narrow pipe restricting water flow. The analogy isn't perfect, but it helps.

A closed circuit provides a complete path for current. An open switch breaks that path.
